Questions & answers

A few quick answers before you start summarizing everything.

Do I need to create an account?

No. ConvoClarity works without any sign‑up. Just paste, summarize, and go.

What can I summarize?

Chats, meeting notes, email threads, articles, blog posts, lecture notes — anything that’s plain text.

Is my text stored?

No. Your text is processed to generate the summary and not stored in a database for this demo project.

Can I trust the summary?

Summaries are usually very good at capturing the main points, but always skim important content yourself before making big decisions.
